📋 Background & Rationale

Deep Resolve (Siemens Healthineers) is an AI-powered MRI image reconstruction platform that enables significantly faster scan acquisition while maintaining diagnostic image quality. Our clinical hypothesis is that reduced scan times will translate directly to shorter sedation durations for patients who require anesthesia for MRI.

This study will quantify that benefit, generate peer-reviewable evidence, and inform future technology and resource decisions at our institution.

❓ Primary Research Question
Does implementation of Deep Resolve MRI AI lead to a statistically significant reduction in sedation time (induction to extubation) in patients undergoing sedated MRI at our institution?

🎯 Expected Patient & Institutional Impact

Shorter sedation durations directly reduce anesthetic risk, improve MRI scanner throughput, lower recovery room resource utilization, and decrease overall procedure cost. For pediatric patients in particular, reduced scan time can meaningfully lower cumulative anesthetic exposure.

This study will generate the first institution-specific evidence for this benefit, contributing to the published literature and supporting future technology procurement decisions.
